BEFORE YOU SET SAIL

A little fate.
A lot of possibility.

Spin one field at a time, or quick-generate everything left. Each wheel uses its displayed weights; the wheel lands on the actual selected result.

A world-inspired balance. Humans and ordinary beginnings are common. Famous bloodlines, Conqueror’s Haki and Mythical Zoans are rare. These are authored odds for adventurers, not official population statistics.

Choices connect. Race shapes height and traits; age and faction affect Haki; weapons follow your style. Three swords means three different weapon rolls, each with its own mastery. Canon crews and recruits are filtered by era and faction.

Your alternate timeline. Famous fruits and weapons can become yours even when a canon owner exists. Recruiting a known character changes their allegiance. A famous family gives connections, never automatic abilities. The curated catalog includes reveals through Wano and early Egghead.

Original companions. Generated locally from authored name, race, role and personality pools, without a live AI model. Journey recruits use the same local-generation approach.

A life at sea. After your origin, begin an entirely fate-driven journey. An event takes four months; its follow-up wheels do not add time. Time skips replace that duration. There are no tactical choices: automatic instincts can shift a later outcome by 7 percentage points, with other probabilities rebalanced. Combat has one resolution wheel after opponent and instinct rolls.

Growth and consequences. Skills gain practice toward permanent ranks. Powers, weapons, allies, injuries and age affect combat odds. Loss can mean injury, mercy, capture or death. Captivity changes the event pool until you escape. World events change future danger. Death ends the run, including death from old age.

A story that remembers. Canon encounters use named people and their actual crews or units. Their routes, reactions, power ratings and meetings with you are alternate-world fiction. This is a starting-era cast, not a full simulation of canon chronology. Friends and grudges can return; someone killed in your journey cannot. New powers favor focused practice, injuries favor recovery, and older characters favor knowledge and legacy. Every dream has four milestones with preparation requirements.

Aging. Longevity is game balance, not official canon: most races share a 70-year aging onset / 120-year hard limit; Giants use 220 / 380, Dwarves 100 / 180, Buccaneers 75 / 130. Beyond onset, mortality rises monthly. A time skip includes every month of aging and cannot pass the hard limit.

Keep your character. Autosave uses this browser. Export a JSON backup to move devices. Undo is available during character creation only. Journey results commit immediately, so refreshing cannot reroll fate; a new character clears the current save only after confirmation.
